# Pitch: Space Mission Design Game (Team Tupi Orbit)

About 1 minute when spoken (about 150 words). Speak slowly; pause at each line break.

---

**The problem.**
Every space mission is a puzzle of trade-offs. A better camera means more science, but also more mass, more power, more data and more cost. Students almost never get to feel these trade-offs. They read about orbits in one class and rockets in another, but never put a whole mission together.

**Our solution.**
Space Mission Design Game is a free browser game where you design a complete mission in five steps: pick a goal, build the spacecraft, choose an orbit and a rocket, fly the mission, and read the report.

**Who it is for.**
High school students and their teachers. No install, no login. It works on a school laptop or a phone.

**What players learn.**
Every choice has a consequence, and the game explains why. Your spacecraft is ten kilograms too heavy? The report tells you, in plain words, and shows you a real NASA mission that faced the same problem. And there is no single right answer.

**What's next.**
With more time, we would add team mode for classrooms, more destinations and more real mission data.
